Walking gait cycle

A jointed figure walks in place: opposite arms and legs swing together, while the knees bend and the body rises twice per stride.

On the web: Character loops, onboarding mascots and game prototypes.

Key idea
Offset the far limbs by half a cycle; animate each joint around its attachment point.

Moiré interference

Two fine stripe patterns overlap. A tiny rotation creates broad moving bands, even though neither layer changes its spacing.

On the web: Optical illustrations, poster art and decorative backgrounds. Keep this subtle and avoid high contrast flashing.

Key idea
Overlay two repeating gradients and slowly rotate only the upper layer.

Standing wave with fixed nodes

The endpoints stay still while the middle oscillates. Each point has its own amplitude, but shares the same phase, creating the first mode of a vibrating string.

On the web: Science explainers, string simulations and audio education.

Key idea
Set amplitude to A × sin(π × index / intervals); keep timing identical for every point.

Rolling wheel / cycloid

A point on the rim of a wheel follows the outlined cycloid. One full turn travels exactly one circumference, so the wheel rolls without slipping. The loop resets after one turn.

On the web: Geometry lessons, mechanism diagrams and illustrated journeys.

Key idea
distance = radius × angle (radians); translate the parent and rotate the wheel independently.

Lissajous figure (2:3)

Combine independent horizontal and vertical oscillations. Two horizontal cycles and three vertical cycles produce a repeating woven curve. The outlined curve is the exact sine reference; CSS easing approximates the moving point.

On the web: Signal explainers, generative art and coordinated motion studies.

Key idea
Animate X and Y on nested elements so transforms do not overwrite each other; use durations in a 3:2 ratio.
